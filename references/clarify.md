# references/clarify.md: Grill-Me Intent Alignment & Analytical Contract Protocol

> **Core Axiom**: Without establishing clear decision objectives and constraints, any generated report is an uncontrolled, high-cost hallucination. Analysts or agents must align on business objectives prior to deep slicing.

---

## 1. Grill-Me Interactive Trigger Workflow

When an analytical task is initiated, execute the following protocol:
1. **Halt Blind Computation**: Do not generate final charts or conclusive dashboards immediately.
2. **Extract Context Clues**: Identify whether the user is an OEM product strategist, a public policy maker, an infrastructure investor, or a regional sales director.
3. **Structured Clarification**: Solicit input on 2-3 decisive scoping dimensions using compact multiple-choice questions.
4. **Lock Specification Contract**: Formalize findings into an analytical specification memorandum before proceeding.

---

## 2. Four Archetype Strategic Templates

### Persona A: OEM Executive & Product Strategy
* **Core Pain Points**: Model pricing sweet spot, battery capacity trade-offs, bundled charging installation vs. price discounting.
* **Alignment Library**:
  1. *Primary Focus*: Are you prioritizing profit margin optimization in upper quadrants (Q1) or volume expansion in mass quadrants (Q2)?
  2. *Hardware Roadmaps*: Does next-generation platform planning include standard high-voltage fast charging or bundled residential wallbox installation?
  3. *Horizon*: Is the analysis intended for near-term quarterly marketing allocation or long-term vehicle architecture redesign?

### Persona B: Public Policy & Fiscal Incentive Taskforce
* **Core Pain Points**: Subsidy phaseout mechanisms, deadweight loss prevention, accelerating penetration in lower-income demographics.
* **Alignment Library**:
  1. *Policy KPI*: Is the primary objective maximizing gross EV count or maximizing additionality per fiscal dollar spent?
  2. *Incentive Structure*: Is point-of-sale cash rebates preferred, or residential wallbox installation tax credits?
  3. *Income Caps*: Should high-income households (e.g. >$120k) face progressive subsidy tapering?

### Persona C: Charging Point Operator (CPO) & Infrastructure Investor
* **Core Pain Points**: Station site selection, low charger utilization, urban multi-unit housing charging deficits.
* **Alignment Library**:
  1. *Investment Focus*: Residential overnight charging vs. destination/workplace public fast-charging plazas?
  2. *Elasticity Metrics*: Quantifying how many potential EV buyers convert per 100 residential chargers deployed?

### Persona D: Regional Dealership & Sales Network Director
* **Core Pain Points**: Lead qualification, test-drive conversions, tailored objection handling.
* **Alignment Library**:
  1. *Execution Scope*: Macro channel quota planning or retail salesperson battlecards?
  2. *Product Bundling*: Availability of bundled residential installation packages for high-anxiety leads?
