# references/methods/segmentation-matrix.md: Income-Anxiety 4-Quadrant Strategic Segmentation

> **Core Logic**: EV consumer adoption is not driven by a single demographic index. It reflects a dual-axis interaction between **purchasing power (income)** and **psychological barrier (range anxiety)**. Precise 4-quadrant slicing enables optimal capital and marketing allocation.

---

## 1. Quadrant Definitions & Orthogonal Boundaries

Based on the empirical population ($N = 668,665$), orthogonal axes are established using median annual income ($\approx \$65,000$) and range anxiety levels (Low vs Medium/High):

```
                ▲ Annual Income
                │
     Q3: Infra-Constrained (4.85%) │     Q1: Premium Prime (69.17%)
     High Income × High Anxiety    │  High Income × Low Anxiety
     [Action: Bundle Wallbox]      │  [Action: Premium Trim & Margin]
  ──────────────┼──────────────────────────────▶ Anxiety Mitigation
                │                                (Anxiety: Low)
     Q4: Double-Frozen (0.24%)     │     Q2: Policy-Sensitive (21.52%)
     Low Income × High Anxiety     │  Low Income × Low Anxiety
     [Action: Freeze Ad Spend]     │  [Action: Tiered Subsidy & Finance]
                │
```

## 3. Segment-Specific Commercial Playbook

### Q1: Premium Prime Cohort (High Income × Low Anxiety)
- **Profile**: Affluent households residing in suburban standalone properties with dedicated private driveways and pre-installed Level 2 wallboxes. Daily commute: 20–40 km.
- **Conversion Bottleneck**: None. Baseline buy rate reaches 69.17%.
- **Strategic Imperative**: **Maximize Gross Margin per Unit**.
- **Tactical Actions**:
  - Emphasize long-range performance, dual-motor AWD, and luxury interior packages.
  - Phase out cash discounts; replace with premium service credits (valet charging, airport lounge access).

### Q2: Policy-Sensitive Mass Cohort (Lower Income × Low Anxiety)
- **Profile**: Younger professionals or single-car households with short commutes (<20 km) who charge at workplaces or municipal lots. Highly price sensitive.
- **Conversion Bottleneck**: Acquisition cost and down payment barriers.
- **Strategic Imperative**: **Drive Volume & Market Penetration**.
- **Tactical Actions**:
  - Target sweet spot pricing: \$25,000–\$32,000.
  - Introduce low-interest 60-month financing; pass through all federal/regional subsidies directly.
  - Every \$2,000 subsidy step yields an empirical $+14.8\%$ conversion lift.

### Q3: Infra-Constrained Affluent Cohort (High Income × High Anxiety)
- **Profile**: High earners living in urban multi-family residential towers without assigned charging infrastructure. High cross-city commuting.
- **Conversion Bottleneck**: Severe range anxiety cuts conversion to 4.85%. Price is not the issue; charging access is.
- **Strategic Imperative**: **Neutralize Charging Friction**.
- **Tactical Actions**:
  - Include complimentary residential wallbox permit concierge and installation with vehicle purchase.
  - Offer guaranteed 3-year subscription to dedicated high-speed charging hubs.

### Q4: Double-Frozen Majority (Lower Income × High Anxiety)
- **Profile**: Low-income commuters lacking charging access.
- **Conversion Bottleneck**: Dual veto of budget deficit and range anxiety. Conversion is 0.24%.
- **Strategic Imperative**: **Freeze Ad Spend & Stop Capital Bleed**.
- **Tactical Actions**:
  - Exclude from performance ad targeting; avoid discount dilution until municipal public charging density reaches critical scale.
